# Changelog

## v0.13.0 — 2026-11-02

An online match now waits for you. If every player leaves, the computer no longer plays the match out on its own; it pauses until someone comes back, and picks up on the same turn.

### Changed
- When everyone has left an online match, no computer moves are made and the turn timer stops until a player rejoins. The turn timer then carries on with the time that was left.
- A player who leaves while others are still playing still has their seat taken over by the computer, as before.
- A waiting match is kept for up to 7 days. The privacy page says so.

## v0.12.0 — 2026-11-02

Online matches don't get lost any more. If you close the tab, reload, or your phone drops off, Rejoin match on the main menu takes you back to your seat, and a match now survives the game's server restarting.

### New
- Rejoin match: the main menu shows a Rejoin match button while you're in an online match, and puts you back in the same seat with the game carrying on.
- Reloading the page in the middle of an online match takes you straight back into it.
- A welcome-back message shows when you rejoin; if the match finished while you were away, the game tells you it has ended.

### Changed
- Online matches are kept on the server, so a server restart no longer ends them; the turn timer and any computer player carry on where they left off.
- A match with no moves for 7 days is cleared. The privacy page says so.
- Your saved settings, tutorial progress, New Game choices and words waiting to be sent carry over safely from earlier versions.

## v0.11.2 — 2026-11-01

On a phone, the board now sits in the middle of the screen, and the game's version shows on the main menu. If something ever breaks, the game says so instead of going blank and sends a short anonymous report so it can be fixed. You can turn reports off in Settings.

### New
- The version number shows at the bottom of the main menu and in Settings.
- When the game breaks, a "Something broke" screen with a Reload button appears instead of a blank page.
- Crash reports: an anonymous technical report is sent when something breaks (no name, address or account). You can switch them off in Settings → Privacy → Send crash reports.
- A privacy page (Settings → About → Privacy) says what crash reports hold, what online play keeps and what happens to submitted words.

### Changed
- On an upright phone, the turn's words sit under the top bar and the board sits in the middle of the screen, easier to reach with a thumb.
- Tip bubbles are light with dark words, so they stand out from the board.
- Tutorial tips about your tray now sit below the tray's seeds instead of covering the board's bottom row.
- The installed app opens upright, and full screen on Android holds the phone upright.
- A few sound effects were evened out so none sticks out.
